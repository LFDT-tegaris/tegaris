# Adopters

No public adopter entries have been submitted yet.

If you use Tegaris and have permission to describe that use publicly, open a pull request with the project or organization name, a public link and a short description. State whether the use is an experiment, evaluation or production deployment. Do not list customers or partners without their permission.

## Suggested entry

Provide the organization/project name, a publicly verifiable reference, the relevant Tegaris version where known, the use-case summary and the stated maturity of that use. Explain the scope accurately; participation in a discussion or demonstration is not production adoption.

Maintainers may ask for clarification or a public reference before accepting an entry. Keep confidential architecture, commercial terms and customer information out of the submission. Update or remove an entry when the public information is no longer accurate.
